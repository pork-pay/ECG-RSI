Experiment details and benchmark scores

S7 equally weights four accuracy and three classification F1 scores on a 0–100 scale. The main trajectory rises from 30.15 to 46.61. Mean QA accuracy increases from 34.57% to 56.04%; mean F1 increases from 24.27 to 34.03. Gains are computed before rounding.

Recorded waveform example and full method

The appendix PR example requests 300 ms and remeasures 298 ms, with heart rate 74.6 bpm. It lengthens the PQ segment and compensates in the TP segment to preserve the RR interval. These values follow the paper’s automatic measurement convention.
